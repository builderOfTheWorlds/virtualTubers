> **Status**: DRAFT v1 — full 168-hour schedule for the pilot loop.

## How to read this

**Content class** — the single most important column. It says who is
responsible for that hour.

| Tag | Class | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **[A]** | Authored | Pre-written, plot-bearing, irreversible. Must fire on time. |
| **[S]** | Semi-authored | Fixed purpose, flexible execution. Beat outline exists, agents fill it. |
| **[R]** | Recap | Generated from what actually happened. Non-negotiable daily. |
| **[I]** | Improvised | Agent-generated inside the §2 constraints of the script doc. Zero irreversible plot. |

**Block** — the six-block daily rhythm: Deep Night (00–06), Dawn (06–09),
Day (09–15), Golden (15–18), **PRIME (18–23)**, Late (23–00).

**Hours are local wall-clock**, assuming a US-evening-prime audience. If the
audience skews EU/AU the whole grid rotates — the *shape* holds, the numbers
move.

### Pilot deviation

Loop 1 is the series premiere, so it has **no preceding reset** to inherit
from. Monday 00:00 is a genuine cold start. Every subsequent loop begins at
the *previous* Sunday 22:00 reset, meaning steady-state loops open with
their Deep Night vignettes already 2 hours in. Loop 1's Monday 00:00 block
is therefore slightly longer on setup than the template calls for.

**Production notes** — Monday is the quietest day and carries the least
plot, by design: it is the day most likely to be *nobody's* first day, since
the audience arrives across the whole week. The 20:00 Grovley line is the
only macro content all day and it must not be underlined.

## Weekly rollup

| Class | Hours | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **[A]** Authored | 71 | 42% |
| **[S]** Semi-authored | 46 | 27% |
| **[R]** Recap | 9 | 5% |
| **[I]** Improvised | 42 | 25% |
| **Total** | **168** | 100% |

Authored load came in higher than the script doc's 56-hour estimate (71
hrs), because Tuesday and Sunday are both near-fully authored. Two
consequences worth planning around:

1. **The writing burden is front- and back-loaded.** Tue and Sun account for
   ~30 of the 71 authored hours. Wed–Fri are comparatively cheap.
2. **Beat 4 (Wed–Thu) remains the pressure valve.** If authoring capacity
   runs short, convert Wednesday's 09:00–14:00 and Thursday's 12:00–17:00
   from [S] to [I] and lose nothing structural.

### Macro seeds planted in Loop 1

Four, all deniable, none acknowledged by the cast:

| # | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon 20:00 | Grovley: *"it's been quite some time"* |
| 2 | Tue 04:00 | Dream — fire, a halved ring, the same phrase |
| 3 | Thu 02:00 | Dream — watching themselves from outside |
| 4 | Sat 04:00 | Dream — answering Grovley *before he speaks* |
