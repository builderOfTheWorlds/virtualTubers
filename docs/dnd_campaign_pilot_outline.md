## Assumption flagged for confirmation

The necromancer/phylactery beats you described read as the climax of the
loop's central quest — the world-ending threat the duke sends the party
after. The existing backstory says that threat is **the antagonist's escape
attempt, framed to the cast as an attempt to destroy the world**, which they
end up thwarting without realizing his real goal. This outline assumes the
**necromancer is that framing** — i.e. the BBEG the party fights this loop
*is* the antagonist (appearing in a necromancer/lich guise, which also fits
the "immortal across thousands of years" characterization). If you meant
the necromancer as a separate, lesser villain unrelated to the time-loop
antagonist, say so and the back half of this outline changes.

## Beat-by-beat outline

| # | Beat | What happens | Est. runtime |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open** | Each PC gets a summons to the duke's castle, shown as separate intro vignettes from their own (believed-orphan) lives. Ends with all PCs converging at the gates. | 2–3 hrs |
| 2 | **The gathering & the call** | Duke's court: the threat is laid out — a necromancer is going to destroy the world. No blood-tie reveal yet (a knowing look from the duke, held for later). Party provisions/equips in the castle town — soft intro to mechanics and chat interaction. | 3–4 hrs |
| 3 | **Sage seed** | Party crosses paths with (or hears rumor of) the current sage — a hook, not a payoff. Plants the character without spending the full mid-loop knowledge beat yet. | 1–2 hrs |
| 4 | **The road (rising action)** | Multi-leg overland journey toward the necromancer's lair. 2–3 distinct legs, each its own mini set-piece (ambush, haunted crossroads, a cursed town, a chat-voted fork). This is the cheapest lever for stretching runtime — legs can be added or cut without touching the spine. | 10–14 hrs (flexible) |
| 5 | **Seeking the sage** | Party detours to the sage properly. Two-stage lore unlock triggers here: partial memory-infusion, cryptic true context on the necromancer, without revealing the loop itself. Good spot to seed antagonist-decay foreshadowing once that's designed. | 3–5 hrs |
| 6 | **Approach & infiltration** | Reaching the lair — guardians, traps, environmental storytelling about the necromancer's true age. Dungeon-crawl density, good exercise for combat/puzzle primitives. | 6–8 hrs |
| 7 | **Confronting the necromancer** | Boss encounter/climax. Dramatic-irony beat: he protests he isn't trying to destroy anything, he's trying to *leave* — cast doesn't grasp the significance. | 3–4 hrs |
| 8 | **Aftermath — the phylactery** | Recover the phylactery, travel to the nearest church, destroy it. Public celebration / duke's court reaction. Natural spot for the duke's-progeny reveal if you want it mid-loop rather than saved for a later cycle. | 2–3 hrs |
| 9 | **Loop reset** | The reset event itself. Whoever's anointed becomes next loop's sage-designate (mechanism still open per the concept doc). Cut to reset transition, week ends. | 1–2 hrs |

## Runtime rollup

Authored total: **~31–45 hours** against a 168-hour week.

This matches the gap already flagged in the brainstorm notes ("there may
not be enough material yet to fill a full week of 24/7 stream"). Options,
none chosen yet:

- **Pad beat 4** (the road) further — it's the one beat designed to flex,
  but even generously stretched it likely can't cover ~120+ hours alone.
- **Add procedural/downtime content** between beats — camp scenes, chat
  Q&A with characters, recap/highlight segments — that isn't part of the
  authored spine but fills stream hours.
- **Accept a short pilot loop** and let something else (rerun, a
  documentary-style recap format, a second short story) fill the remainder
  of week one while later loops get built out to full length.

## Where existing mechanics hook in

- Beat 3 and 5 are where the sage's **two-stage knowledge unlock** actually
  fires (seed, then real infusion) — see `story_time_loop_concept.md`.
- Beat 9 is where the **sage handoff** (two characters, outgoing +
  incoming) needs to physically happen on stream.
- The **wipe contingency** (parked) would slot in as a branch off beats
  6–7 if the party goes down — no design yet, flagged in the concept doc.
