# The Bahadur

The Bahadur come from Ord, a world in a future timeline that has already lost
all of its magic. Ten thousand years ago their leadership crossed into
Ashiorid at the Event and got trapped here when the crossing closed behind
them. Everyone present that day, Bahadur included, came out of it immortal.

They spent the ten thousand years since breeding two things: creatures that
open microfractures in spacetime, and people who can absorb and store enough
raw energy to force those fractures wider. The second breeding program is why
magic is so rare in Ashiorid now — not natural scarcity, but ten millennia of
it being harvested to fund a door home.

Their Sultan, Muad'Malik, took a head wound at the Event that curdled into
something worse over the centuries. He has mostly stopped caring about Ord
and spends his immortality on necromancy and worse breeding work. Some of his
own leadership are still loyal to the original plan. Some have quietly broken
from him. Nothing about the Bahadur is unified, which is the only reason
Ashiorid has lasted this long.
