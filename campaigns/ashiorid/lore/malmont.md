# Malmont

Malmont is a town of a little over five thousand people, four days south of
where Leto's manor stood before the fire. It runs two magic shops for a town
its size, which is one more than the arithmetic says it should support, and
its four blacksmiths keep their forges cold more often than a town this
prosperous has any reason to.

Two power centers share Malmont uneasily: a lawful constable's watch that
runs the streets in daylight, and something unregistered and unfriendly that
moves through the town at night. Nobody who lives there names the second one
out loud.

It is the first real town on the road south, and the first place since the
manor burned where the four of you are complete strangers — which, after a
week of people already knowing your names, is its own kind of relief.
