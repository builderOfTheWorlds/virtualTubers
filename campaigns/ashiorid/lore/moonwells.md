# Moonwells

Moonwells are where the world's magic pools. They are not built and cannot be
dug; they are found. Drawing from one is drawing from a shared account, which
is why the Age of War ended the way it did.

A drained well goes grey and stays grey. Nobody has ever refilled one.

The last well is the one the Event happened at. It is also, per Grovley, not
unrelated to what is held in the subbasement of Leto's manor — though he will
not say how, and the players should not get a straight answer this arc.
