# The Lightning Scar

A burn on the left side of Harry Potter's forehead, in the shape of a
lightning bolt, sustained as an infant when You-Know-Who's killing curse
rebounded off him and killed You-Know-Who instead.

It has not pained Harry in nineteen years by the end of the source corpus.
Before that it ached when You-Know-Who was near, and he could use it once
to see through someone else's eyes.

The scar is the reason the Dursleys were afraid of him and the reason
Hogwarts took him in. It is also the reason every Muggle newspaper calls
him the Boy You-Know-Who Killed.
