# The Basement

The Cyber Police work out of a basement corridor under the department's main building, reached by
a service stairwell most of the rest of the building has forgotten exists. The floor above has
windows. This one does not.

## Layout, front to back

**The door.** Frosted glass, stencilled DIGITAL CRIMES TASK FORCE in lettering nobody has
refreshed in years. Someone, once, added a small sticker of a skull-and-crossbones beside it. It
has never been removed.

**The Bullpen.** The main room. Six desks in a loose horseshoe around a shared bank of monitors
mounted on the wall, most of them showing live dashboards nobody fully trusts.

- The **Detective** has the desk nearest the door, so she's the first one anyone from upstairs
  talks to, which she has stopped minding.
- The **Analyst** sits surrounded by printouts, link charts and three monitors, in a nest of cable
  ties the Quartermaster has given up fighting.
- The **Agent** has the desk with the most equipment on it: a rack of hardware, two laptops that
  are never both working, and a drawer that locks.
- **Forensics** sits in the corner nearest the evidence locker, behind a line of tape on the
  floor nobody but Forensics is allowed to cross without gloves.
- **Press** has the desk nearest the stairs, so she's the first one out when a camera crew shows
  up, and the only one who keeps a tie in a drawer.
- The last desk in the horseshoe is bare except for a department-issue phone that rings once a
  year, always from Internal Affairs, always a wrong number.

**The Captain's office.** A glass-walled box at the back, visible from every desk, the same way
Ashiorid's Glass Box is. The glass is frosted at desk height "for warrants," which fools nobody.

**The Evidence Locker.** A caged room behind Forensics' desk, keypad-locked, holding drives,
servers and the paperwork chain of custody that keeps all of it usable in court. Forensics and the
Captain are the only two with the code.

**The Comms Room.** A small room with a bank of radios nobody uses any more and a single modern
console the unit actually relies on. It doubles as the interview room when a suspect is brought
in, which does not happen often enough for anyone to mind the overlap.

**The Supply Cage.** The Quartermaster's domain: a locked cage of hardware, cables, go-bags and
the requisition binder. Nothing leaves without a signature.

**The Observer's corner.** A single desk by the stairwell door, turned to face the bullpen instead
of a monitor. It has one chair, one closed folder, and no computer. Nobody remembers it being
assigned. It is the only desk with a clear view of every monitor in the room and of the Captain's
office glass.

## After hours

The unit keeps standard department hours, 07:00 to 19:00, unlike Ashiorid's round-the-clock floor
— but The Scuttle doesn't keep hours at all, and the unit's pagers go off on its schedule, not
theirs. See `rituals.md`.
