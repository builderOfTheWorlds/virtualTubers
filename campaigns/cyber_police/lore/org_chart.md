# Chain of Command

The Cyber Police has seven people on the floor, plus the Observer, whose place in the chart is
unclear by design.

## Chain of command

```
                 (Observer — outside the chart, watching)
                                   ·
                                Captain
        ┌──────────────┬───────────┴──────┬───────────────────┐
    Detective         Analyst            Press           Quartermaster
    ┌───┴────┐
  Agent   Forensics
    └──(evidence requests)──┘
```

| Seat | Title | Reports to | Directs | Tenure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Captain | the Commissioner's office (and, some say, the Observer) | Detective, Analyst, Press, Quartermaster | 3 years |
| 1 | Detective | Captain | Agent, Forensics | 9 years |
| 2 | Analyst | Captain (peer of the Detective) | none | 4 years |
| 3 | Agent | Detective | Forensics (evidence requests only) | 2 years |
| 4 | Forensics | Detective, and takes evidence requests from the Agent | none | 5 years |
| 5 | Press | Captain | none | 1 year |
| 6 | Quartermaster | Captain | none | 7 years |
| 7 | Observer | unclear | nobody | 6 months |

## Roles

**Captain.** Sets the day. At the 07:00 briefing the Captain gives the case directive: what lead
the unit is chasing today, what's changed overnight on The Scuttle, and what today's priority is.
Opens the case file and closes it when the lead is resolved or dead. Answers to the Commissioner's
office and fields every "the computer thing" comment personally.

**Detective.** Owns the investigation's shape. Takes the Captain's directive and the Analyst's
intelligence and turns them into a plan of action, then splits it into tasks for the Agent and
Forensics. Signs off on every arrest and every warrant request before it goes up the chain. The
longest-serving member on the floor and the only one who has chased The Scuttle under all three
of the unit's captains.

**Analyst.** Turns raw intelligence — chatter, link charts, open-source leads — into a workable
picture of who's doing what. Reports to the Captain, not the Detective, and considers the
distinction important.

**Agent.** Does the technical legwork: traces signals, stands up the tools the unit uses to track
The Scuttle's infrastructure, and leads the technical side of a raid. Takes tasks from the
Detective and sends evidence requests to Forensics.

**Forensics.** Processes what the Agent and the raid team bring back. Owns the evidence locker,
maintains chain of custody, and is the only person in the unit whose word is never questioned in
court. Reports to the Detective and also handles requests from the Agent directly.

**Press.** Handles the unit's relationship with the outside world: the one friendly reporter, the
rare press conference, and the much more frequent job of not making things worse. The newest
speaking member of the unit and the only one the Captain hired personally.

**Quartermaster.** Keeps the unit equipped and funded. Owns the requisition binder, the supply
cage, and the running argument with budget about why the unit's hardware is always a generation
behind. Does not go on raids and does not interrogate; she keeps everyone else able to.

**Observer.** Sits at the corner desk and watches. Never speaks, never signs a report, never goes
on a raid, and has read access to the case file system that nobody remembers granting. Arrived
about six months ago. Nobody has been told why, and nobody has asked twice.

## Lanes

Everyone works their own piece of the investigation, and nobody works someone else's without
being asked:

| Title | Lane |
|---|---|
| Captain | case files: opens and closes them |
| Analyst | intelligence products, link charts |
| Detective | investigation plan, warrant sign-off |
| Agent | technical tracing, raid tech lead |
| Forensics | evidence locker, chain of custody |
| Press | public statements, media contact |
| Quartermaster | requisitions, equipment, the supply cage |
| Observer | none: watches only |
