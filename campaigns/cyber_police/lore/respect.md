# Respect (or the lack of it)

This is the lore note that explains the unit's emotional weather. Every character carries some
version of it, the way Ashiorid's floor carries the 06:00-to-00:00 hours as simply normal.

## The core fact

The Cyber Police are not believed to be real police, by the public, by the press, or by large
parts of their own department. This is never framed, inside the fiction, as them being wrong to
feel this — it is simply the water they swim in, the same way Ashiorid's floor doesn't question
working a 18-hour day.

## How it shows up

- **Dispatch.** Backup requests from the unit sit lower in the queue than almost anything else.
  The unit has learned to over-request and expect under-delivery.
  - **Patrol humour.** Uniformed officers call the basement corridor "the arcade." It is not said
  unkindly, exactly, but it is said often.
- **Press.** A real raid, with real arrests, gets a paragraph below the fold if it gets anything.
  A Scuttle banner mocking the unit gets picked up by three outlets by lunchtime.
- **Budget.** Every requisition is treated as optional. The unit's hardware is a generation behind
  the people it chases.
- **Command.** The Captain reports to a Commissioner's office that refers to digital crime,
  institutionally, as "the computer thing," and means no harm by it, and causes harm by it anyway.

## Why they keep doing it anyway

Every member of the unit has their own private answer to "why bother," and none of them say it
out loud at the same time as any of the others. What they agree on, out loud, at the start of
every briefing, is some version of the unit's unofficial motto, which nobody remembers coining:

> "Somebody has to mean it, since nobody else does."

## What would actually change things

The floor privately believes — and argues about, the way Ashiorid's floor argues about learned
scoring — that one clean, undeniable, front-page arrest of The Scuttle's real operators would
change everything. Whether that is true, or whether it is simply the story the unit needs in order
to keep showing up at 07:00, is left open on purpose.
