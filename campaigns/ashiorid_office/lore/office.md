# The Office

Ashiorid occupies the whole third floor of the Tallow Street building, a narrow brick block with
a lift that works and a stairwell that everyone uses anyway. The floor is one long rectangle, with
windows along the street side and the service corridor along the back. Everything described below
is on that one floor.

## Floor plan, front to back

**Reception.** A small lobby at the top of the stairs, with a desk that has had no receptionist
since the founder's time. The Office Manager keeps the visitor book, the key cabinet and the post
tray here. A framed copy of the company values hangs on the wall. The box of Oswin Harrowgate's
unopened mail sits in the bottom drawer.

**The Glass Box.** The CEO's office, directly off reception, walled in glass on two sides. The
blinds have never been fully closed by any of the three CEOs. There is a pale rectangle on the
back wall where the founder's whiteboard hung. The current CEO has hung nothing over it.

**The Moonwell room.** The one proper meeting room. It has a long table, eight chairs, a
screen that takes two minutes to wake, and the Fraud-Stop roadmap pinned to the cork wall. The
founder named it after "a place his family used to talk about," and nobody has thought to rename
it. The Friday demo happens here. So did the second CEO's last phone call.

**The Open Floor.** The main working area, running along the windows. Six desks in two
facing rows of three:

- The **Tech Lead** has the window desk nearest the Glass Box, with two monitors, one of them
  permanently showing the latency graph. It is the only desk on the floor that has not moved in
  eight years.
- The **Engineer** faces the Tech Lead, close enough to be asked things without anyone getting up.
- The **Tester** sits beside the Engineer, with a spare laptop that runs the test suite without stopping.
- The **Analyst** sits beside the Tech Lead. Their desk is buried in printouts of client
  requirements, annotated in three colours.
- **Marketing** has the desk at the far end, nearest the kitchen, with a small whiteboard of
  pipeline figures and a plant nobody else is allowed to water.
- The last desk in the row is empty. It belonged to an engineer who left under the second CEO, and
  it is used for spare chairs and parcels.

**The Party Member's desk.** In the back corner of the Open Floor, beside the fire exit, set
apart from the two rows and turned to face into the room instead of toward a window. It has one
chair, one closed notebook, one pen, and no computer. It is the only desk on the floor with a clear
view of every screen, and also of the Glass Box. Nobody remembers it being delivered. The Office
Manager cleans around it and does not move anything on it.

**The Kitchen.** Behind the Open Floor. This was the original office, where the first four hires
shared one table, and the table is still there. The kitchen has:

- the coffee machine (a large, temperamental espresso unit that only the Office Manager can
  descale)
- a fridge with a strictly enforced labelling policy
- a snack shelf restocked daily
- the bins, which are sorted into four streams and taken out at 23:30 without exception

Lunch is eaten here (see `rituals.md`). The laminated values card is taped by the door.

**The Office Manager's station.** A counter and a tall cupboard at the kitchen's back door,
opening onto the service corridor. It holds cleaning supplies, the first-aid kit, the HR files in
a locked drawer, the snack stock, and a ring of keys that is larger than the floor seems to need.
It is also where the main light switches and the floor's breaker panel are.

**The Small Room.** A windowless phone booth with one chair, used for calls, interviews and
the occasional private conversation that everyone can half-hear anyway.

**The Server Closet.** At the far back end of the service corridor, behind a heavy door with a
keypad. Fraud-Stop's production traffic runs in a data centre, but the closet still holds:

- the office network gear
- the build machine
- a rack running the staging environment
- one unlabelled grey box that nobody will admit to owning

The closet hums at a steady note that people stop hearing after the first week. It is kept cold,
and in summer it is the most popular place on the floor. The Tech Lead and the Office Manager both
know the keypad code. The current CEO had to ask.

## After hours

At 00:00 the Office Manager turns off the floor lights from her station (see `rituals.md`). The
emergency lights stay on over the fire exit. That means the Party Member's corner is the one part
of the floor that is never fully dark. The server closet keeps humming through the night.
