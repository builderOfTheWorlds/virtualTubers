# Hogwarts

A castle perched over a valley in the Scottish highlands. The only school
for witches and wizards in Britain. It is built around four towers, one
per house, and the moving staircase on the staircase that leads to the
Great Hall is the only permanent staircase to the fifth floor.

The Sorting Hat assigns first-year students to houses on the first night
of term. House points are accumulated during the year; the house with the
highest total at the end wins the House Cup and its members are awarded a
candlestick for the following year.
